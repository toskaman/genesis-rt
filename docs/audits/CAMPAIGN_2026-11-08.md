## Incidents and fixes (both operational, not product)

1. **`boot` kills its QEMU at `--timeout`** (found 04:05).
   `subprocess.run(timeout=...)` terminates the child, so the
   900 s smoke check ended with a dead VM and phase 1 aborted
   in `wait-ssh`. Fix: smoke transcript kept as the boot
   record; the live VM was started detached with the
   verbatim recorded cmdline. Repo fix committed: quickstart
   §4 + codelab Lab 6 now document the terminating smoke
   check, the detached-launch recipe, and the `login:`-marker
   gate (the forwarded port accepts TCP long before sshd
   is up — a premature phase-2 launch proved it).
2. **Overlapping phase-2 instances wiped `/tmp/genesis`
   mid-verify** (found 04:14). Two `run-campaign-phase2`
   shells overlapped; killing the parents orphaned a `run`
   python whose retry connected mid-matrix and executed the
   auth-probe cleanup (`rm -rf /tmp/genesis`). Phase-3
   result: 10/16 PASS then 6 missing-file FAILs — every
   check that ran with files present passed, including
   faultinject 9/9 and device_chain NONGUARD=0. Fix: single
   instance (phase 4), full green. Lesson recorded: never
   kill a campaign launcher without its children; the
   auth probe should stay side-effect-free (follow-up).
